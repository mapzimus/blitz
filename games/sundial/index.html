SUNDIAL

BRING YOUR OWN DISC

This page ships no game data. It reads the archives straight off your own copy of the disc, in your browser, and keeps them in this browser only.

  1. Copy your disc to a folder (for example Downloads\ts2_disc).
  2. Click below and pick that folder (it holds PAK and MUSIC). Picking just PAK works too, without music.
  3. Pick an arena. Click the screen to grab the mouse. WASD move, mouse look, click fire, wheel / 1-9 switch weapon, R reload, right-click zoom, Space jump, Tab scores, Esc release.
